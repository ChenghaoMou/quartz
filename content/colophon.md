This blog started at a normal [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) website with my [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)notes. Over the time, I have mofified it with some additional features:
- [Flexoki](https://stephango.com/flexoki) color themes
- Sidenotes inspired from [Tufte CSS](https://edwardtufte.github.io/tufte-css/) and [Aaron's notes](https://aarnphm.xyz/)
- Layout changes to keep it minimal^[Mostly getting rid of left and right panels and graph visualization]

Most of the [frontend code]^[Fork is available at [ChenghaoMou/quartz](https://github.com/ChenghaoMou/quartz)] changes, if not all, are from Claude in [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) or [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code), and built with Cloudflare workers.
